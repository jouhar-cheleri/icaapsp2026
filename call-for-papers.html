Call for Papers

Call for Papers

We invite authors to submit original and unpublished research papers that explore innovative ideas across various topics covered in ICAAP & SP 2026. The acceptance of papers will depend on the evaluation by the peer review committee and authors will receive notifications accordingly. Authors are requested to submit their abstracts in format given above. Authors must present their papers at the conference.

Only the registered participants are requested to submit their full papers to the email address provided. These papers will undergo peer review by experts in relevant fields. Please note that authors are responsible for covering the article processing charges for publication. Instructions regarding full paper submission will be updated.

Publication

Selected papers will be considered for Publication in reputed SCI indexed Q1/Q2 journals.

Publication costs, if any, will be borne by the authors or their institutions.
